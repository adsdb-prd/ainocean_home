BLAD × Ainocean Global HQ Internship: Call for Applications

Ainocean, an AI infrastructure company, is looking for interns to join us at our global headquarters (HQ), the BLAD Irvine office. Throughout the internship, you will work alongside our HQ engineering and back-office teams and learn firsthand how a global AI infrastructure company operates. If you are ready to take the first step in your career on the global stage, we look forward to your application.

  1. Eligibility: as of the posting date, meet at least one of the following
    • Born between 1999 and 2006
    • Currently enrolled in or on leave from a university
    • Graduated from a university within the past 12 months
  2. Positions: up to 4 (tentative)
  3. Internship period: 3 weeks (19 days), Monday, January 11 – Friday, January 29, 2027
    Weekdays, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM
  4. Location: Ainocean Global HQ, BLAD Irvine Office
    500 Technology, Suite 320, Irvine, CA 92618, USA
  5. Housing: details will be shared with selected candidates
  6. Selection schedule:
    Application periodMonday, November 16 – Sunday, November 22, 2026
    Phone interviewMonday, November 23 – Tuesday, November 24, scheduled individually
    Results announcedWednesday, November 25, 2026
    Applicants who pass the document review will be invited to a brief phone interview of about 10 minutes.
  7. How to apply: use the application link below
  8. Please note
    • This internship is unpaid, in accordance with the U.S. Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA).
    • Housing is provided. All other costs and activities during your stay are not covered.
    • Selected interns must arrange their own ESTA, passport, and flights. Those who cannot do so will not be able to participate.
    • Each intern’s assignment will be determined based on local conditions and the results of the document review and phone interview.
  9. Contact: Jessie Yook, Business Operations Manager, Ainocean (+82 10-2494-2920)
