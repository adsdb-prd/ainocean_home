BLAD × Ainocean Global Career Lab 2027: Call for Participants

Ainocean, an AI infrastructure company, invites you to Global Career Lab, a short-term educational workshop at our global headquarters (HQ), the BLAD Irvine office. Throughout the workshop, you will learn from our HQ engineering and operations teams through a series of sessions and see firsthand how a global AI infrastructure company operates. If you are eager to explore career paths on the global stage, we look forward to your application.

  1. Who can apply: applicants must meet at least one of the following as of the posting date
    • Currently enrolled in or on leave from a university
    • Graduated from a university within the past 12 months
  2. Participants: up to 4
  3. Workshop period: 3 weeks, Monday, January 11 – Friday, January 29, 2027
    Core sessions: Weekdays only
  4. Location: Ainocean Global HQ, BLAD Irvine Office
    500 Technology, Suite 320, Irvine, CA 92618, USA
  5. Accommodation: details will be shared with selected participants
  6. Selection schedule:
    Application periodTuesday, November 17 – Sunday, November 22, 2026
    Phone callMonday, November 23 – Tuesday, November 24, 2026, scheduled individually
    Results announcedWednesday, November 25, 2026
    Shortlisted applicants will be invited to a brief phone call of about 10 minutes.
  7. How to apply: use the application link below
  8. Please note
    • This is a non-credit educational workshop. Participants will not receive wages or any other compensation.
    • Accommodation, transportation to and from the workshop, and breakfast and lunch on workshop days are provided. Flights and personal living expenses are covered by each participant.
    • Participants must obtain their own travel documents (such as visas) before departure; guidance will be provided.
    • Weekends are free for participants’ own plans; no activities are organized.
    • Participation in this workshop does not lead to or guarantee employment.
    • Ainocean and BLAD do not discriminate on the basis of age, sex, race, or any other protected characteristic.
  9. Contact: Jessie Yook, Business Operations Manager, Ainocean (+82 10-2494-2920)
