MODEL 1

Score Maximization Engine

In production

Study time is finite. Model 1 finds the shortest path from where a student is to the score they need.

  1. 01 Diagnosis
  2. 02 Prediction
  3. 03 Recommendation
  • Ultra-fast diagnosisFive adaptive questions. That is all it takes to place a student accurately.
  • Optimal learning pathIt serves the problems that move the score most, minute for minute.
  • Proven foundationRiiid’s Knowledge Space Theory and deep learning models, already tested at scale.

Three to five questions predict a 75. Then comes the practice that reaches 89, and the reason it works.

MODEL 2

Conversational LLM Tutor

Next generation · Socratic dialogue

A score is a start, not the point. Model 2 teaches the concept, one question at a time, whenever a teacher is out of reach.

  1. 01 Curriculum RAG
  2. 02 Model alignment
  3. 03 Evolution
  • Curriculum RAG indexingGrounded in Paraguay’s own textbooks and national curriculum. Accurate, and safe by design.
  • Teacher-in-the-loop RLHFTuned on feedback from Paraguayan teachers who are in classrooms right now.
  • Continuous evolutionFrom rules to reasoning, one release at a time.

A tutor built for the Paraguayan classroom. Not adapted to it.

MODEL 3

Agentic AI Tutor

Future vision · Autonomous agent

A co-teacher that does not wait to be asked. Model 3 sets the goal, does the work, and checks in on its own.

  1. 01 Goal planning
  2. 02 Tool execution
  3. 03 Proactive coaching
  • Multi-step goal decompositionA full term of curriculum, broken into weekly milestones for each student.
  • Early misconception detectionIt reads hesitation. And steps in before a mistake becomes a habit.
  • Human teacher copilotThe moment a student is truly stuck, the teacher knows.

An agent that carries the whole arc of learning.
